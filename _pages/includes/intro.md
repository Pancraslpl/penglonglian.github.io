- I am currently a lecturer in the Department of Automation, School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Wuhan Polytechnic University. I received my Ph.D. degree in June 2026 from the School of Automation Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), under the supervision of Prof. Shicai Fan. My research interests include continual learning, domain adaptation and generalization, causal representation learning, open-set recognition, and few-shot learning, with a particular focus on intelligent fault diagnosis and prognostics and health management (PHM) in complex industrial environments.

- Prior to my doctoral studies, I received my M.Sc. degree in Control Engineering from Xi'an University of Technology and my B.Eng. degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Northwest Minzu University. I have participated in collaborative research projects with Dongfang Electric and the China Academy of Engineering Physics, addressing practical challenges in hydropower units, turbine digital electro-hydraulic (DEH) control systems, embedded sensing modules, and UAV swarm communication systems.

- My research has resulted in publications in international journals, including Advanced Engineering Informatics, Reliability Engineering & System Safety, Control Engineering Practice, and Measurement, as well as papers presented at international IEEE conferences. I have received several academic honors and awards, including First-Class Scholarships, Outstanding Thesis Awards, and multiple Outstanding Teaching Assistant Awards.
